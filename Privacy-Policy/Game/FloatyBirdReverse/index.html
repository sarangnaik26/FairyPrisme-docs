Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 07, 2026

Floaty Bird Reverse ("us", "we", or "our") operates the Floaty Bird Reverse mobile application (the "Service").

Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information from you while you use our Service. We do not require any registration, login, or personal profile details.

Local Data Storage

To enhance your experience, the Service saves your game progress (high scores, coin balance, and unlocked birds) locally on your device using "shared_preferences". This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers or any third-party providers.

Third-Party Services

The Service may use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, particularly for serving personalized advertisements. Link to the privacy policy of third-party service providers used by the app:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in our Service. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's Advertising ID and cookies to provide personalized ads. Depending on your region (e.g., the European Economic Area or UK), you will be asked to provide explicit consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change or revoke this consent at any time via the app's settings or your device settings.

Log Data

We want to inform you that whenever you use our Service, in a case of an error in the app we may collect data and information (through third-party products) on your phone called Log Data. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

Children's Privacy and Age Gate

Our Service is designed to be safe for all ages. Upon first launching the app, we present a neutral age screen to determine the user's age locally. For users under the age of 16, we explicitly configure our third-party advertising services (Google AdMob) to only serve non-personalized, age-appropriate advertisements (Maximum Content Rating G) and we disable all personalized ad tracking signals. We do not collect or request any personally identifiable information from any user, including children. The age information you enter is only stored locally on your device to apply the correct privacy settings and is never transmitted to our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
